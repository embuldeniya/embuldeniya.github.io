The Problem

Clinicians spend their day writing, not treating

In busy outpatient clinics, every minute spent on notes is a minute away from patients. The tools that exist were not designed for the languages, workflows or budgets of South Asian healthcare.

01

Documentation burden

A typical outpatient note takes around 8 minutes to write. Across a full clinic list, that's hours of physician time lost every day — and a leading driver of burnout.

02

The language gap

Consultations switch fluidly between Sinhala, Tamil and English. English-first AI scribes break on code-switched speech, leaving these markets underserved.

03

Fragmented, paper-heavy records

Many practices still run on paper, handwritten lab reports and disconnected tools — so patient history, safety checks and follow-up are hard to get right.

Why now
  • Speech & language models can now handle low-resource and code-switched languages at clinical quality.
  • WhatsApp is the default channel for patients — a zero-install front door for intake and follow-up.
  • Cloud costs have collapsed, making AI affordable at emerging-market price points.
