01

Clinical Structure Modeling

Enforces strict SOAP-compliant clinical note organization (Chief Complaint, History, Examination, Investigations, Assessment, Plan) automatically across all consultations.

02

Sri Lankan Medical Lexicon

Equipped with a curated domain dictionary containing 300+ locally used pharmaceutical brand names, investigations, and clinical phrases across Sinhala, Tamil, and English.

03

Safety-First Guardrails

Deterministic checks verify drug-drug interactions, known allergies, and dosing boundaries before any documentation is rendered for review.

nestor_clinical_engine.py 
# Ambient consultation stream ingestion
capture_stream = nestor.ambient_audio(
    sources=["physician_mic", "patient_mic"],
    languages=["si-LK", "ta-LK", "en-LK"]
)

# Trilingual transcription & entity recognition
raw_dialogue = transcribe_code_switched(capture_stream)
clinical_entities = nestor.extract_entities(
    vocab_profile="sl_medical_pharmacopoeia"
)

# Generate structured SOAP note
soap_record = nestor.synthesize_soap(
    dialogue=raw_dialogue,
    guardrails={
        "flag_interactions": True,
        "verify_allergies": True
    }
)

NESTOR.AI: A Native AI Physician Assistant for Multilingual Clinical Documentation in Sri Lanka

Authors: Dr. Helika Illangakoon & Tharinda Embuldeniya · Aurora Labs (Pvt) Ltd

NESTOR.AI is a locally developed AI Physician Assistant designed for Sri Lankan outpatient settings. The platform utilises multilingual speech recognition and locally deployed AI, incorporating locally aligned clinical prompts, to convert doctor–patient conversations into structured medical documentation. It supports Sinhala, Tamil, and English consultations, generating clinical notes, prescriptions, referral letters, and searchable digital records, while providing AI-driven clinical decision support and drug safety alerts.
